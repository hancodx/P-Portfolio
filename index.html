Architectures for conversational agents on messaging platforms, and the trade-offs between cost, latency, reliability and privacy.

Adapting language models

LLMs for customer analytics

Combining unsupervised segmentation with LLM-based generation for personalized communication at scale.

Privacy-aware, deployable AI

Production-oriented systems built on microservices, message queues and containers, designed with data protection in mind.
